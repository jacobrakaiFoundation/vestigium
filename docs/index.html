LOCAL PROTOTYPE

PASSAIC COUNTY DEMONSTRATION

A local workspace for the office. Not a replacement for the court.

Vestigium is a local court-operations prototype for county, surrogate, and clerk-style work in probate and General Equity. It is not connected to NJ eCourts or any live court system.

This is a local prototype. The demonstration uses fictional records. Dates are entered by staff. The application source remains private.

WHO IT IS FOR

Office work, as the office already does it.

Staff who keep a queue, intake, and numbering outside the core Judiciary systems — spreadsheets, paper, and local procedure. The first demonstration is framed for Passaic County probate and General Equity. It does not assume every county works the same way.

WHAT IT IS

A workspace kept on the office’s machine.

Vestigium holds a work queue, reviewed intake, office numbering, and parties or estate facts the staff record. It is meant to sit beside existing practice until an office has an approved source and a reason to connect further.

WHAT IT IS NOT

Not eCourts. Not a public case file.

  • No connection to NJ eCourts or county filing systems
  • No calculated court-rule deadlines, holidays, or tolling
  • No live records — sample and staff-entered data only
  • Source, API, and case workspace are not published here

This is a local prototype

The case application is not available on this site. The repository that contains it is private. There is no public API to call and no invitation to run court records on the open internet.
